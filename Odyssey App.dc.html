STAGE 1 · STUDY
Contracts of Employment

A contract of employment is the agreement — written, oral or implied — under which an employee undertakes to work for an employer in return for wages. Under the Nigerian Labour Act, the employer must issue written particulars of the contract within three months of engagement.

KEY TERM
Written particulars
The statutory statement of terms: names of parties, nature of work, wage rate, hours, and notice periods.
EXAM TIP
Learn the three-month rule and the minimum notice periods by length of service — they appear in nearly every past paper.

The contract may be for a fixed term, for a specified task, or of indefinite duration. Termination rights differ sharply across these three forms, and examiners frequently test that distinction.
